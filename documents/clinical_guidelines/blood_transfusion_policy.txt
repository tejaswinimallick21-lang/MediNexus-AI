DOCUMENT TITLE: Safe Blood Transfusion Policy
DOCUMENT ID: POL-CLIN-010
REVISION: 1.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-06-01

1. CONSENT AND INDICATION
Transfusion requires documented consent and a documented indication. For stable adult inpatients a restrictive threshold of haemoglobin 70 g/L is used, or 80 g/L with cardiovascular disease, unless clinically justified otherwise.

2. BEDSIDE CHECK
Two qualified staff independently confirm patient identity against the wristband and the blood component label at the bedside immediately before starting.

3. OBSERVATIONS
Record temperature, pulse, blood pressure and respiratory rate before the transfusion, 15 minutes after starting and at the end.

4. SUSPECTED REACTION
Stop the transfusion immediately, keep the IV line open with saline, call the physician, and return the bag to the blood bank. Report every reaction to the haemovigilance team.
