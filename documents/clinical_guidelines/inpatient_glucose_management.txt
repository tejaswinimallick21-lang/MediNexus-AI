DOCUMENT TITLE: Inpatient Diabetes and Glucose Management
DOCUMENT ID: CPG-CLIN-008
REVISION: 1.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-06-01

1. MONITORING
Patients with diabetes have capillary blood glucose checked before meals and at bedtime, or every 4-6 hours if not eating.

2. TARGETS
For most non-critically ill inpatients the target range is 7.8 to 10.0 mmol/L (140 to 180 mg/dL). Tighter targets are reserved for selected patients without hypoglycaemia risk.

3. HYPOGLYCAEMIA
A glucose below 4.0 mmol/L (70 mg/dL) is treated immediately per the hypoglycaemia algorithm and rechecked after 15 minutes. Every episode is reviewed to find the cause.

4. DISCHARGE
Patients started on insulin receive device teaching and a follow-up appointment with the diabetes team within 2 weeks. An HbA1c is measured if none was done in the previous 3 months.
