DOCUMENT TITLE: Medication Reconciliation at Transitions of Care
DOCUMENT ID: CPG-CLIN-007
REVISION: 1.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-06-01

1. PURPOSE
Medication errors are most common when patients move between care settings. Reconciliation compares what the patient was taking with what is prescribed now and resolves every difference.

2. ON ADMISSION
A best possible medication history is taken within 24 hours using at least two sources: the patient or carer, the primary care record, pharmacy dispensing records or the patient's own medicines.

3. AT DISCHARGE
The discharge prescription is reconciled against the admission list and inpatient changes. Every started, stopped or changed medicine is explained to the patient and documented with the reason. High-risk discharges (see CPG-CLIN-001) receive a pharmacist counselling session.

4. MEASURE
Target: 95% of admissions reconciled within 24 hours.
