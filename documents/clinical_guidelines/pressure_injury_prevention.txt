DOCUMENT TITLE: Pressure Injury Prevention Guideline
DOCUMENT ID: CPG-CLIN-009
REVISION: 1.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-06-01

1. RISK ASSESSMENT
Skin inspection and a validated risk score (for example the Braden scale) on admission and daily thereafter. A Braden score of 18 or lower indicates risk.

2. PREVENTION BUNDLE
- Reposition at least every 2 hours for bed-bound patients at risk.
- Pressure-redistributing mattress for high-risk patients.
- Keep skin clean and dry; manage incontinence promptly.
- Screen nutrition and refer to the dietitian when intake is poor.

3. REPORTING
New pressure injuries of stage 2 or above are reported as incidents and reviewed by the tissue viability nurse.
