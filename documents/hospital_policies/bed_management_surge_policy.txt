DOCUMENT TITLE: Bed Management and Surge Escalation Policy
DOCUMENT ID: POL-OPS-003
REVISION: 1.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-06-01

1. DAILY BED MEETING
Bed managers meet at 08:00 and 14:00 to review expected admissions, discharges and blocked beds.

2. SURGE LEVELS
- Level 1 (occupancy below 85%): normal operations.
- Level 2 (85-92%): expedite discharges before 11:00, open the discharge lounge, review elective admissions for the next 48 hours.
- Level 3 (above 92%): activate escalation beds, defer non-urgent elective surgery, daily executive huddle.

3. DISCHARGE BEFORE NOON
Wards aim to discharge 30% of planned discharges before 12:00 to free beds for emergency admissions.
