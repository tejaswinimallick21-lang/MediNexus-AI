DOCUMENT TITLE: Data Retention and Security Incident Response Policy
DOCUMENT ID: POL-IT-002
REVISION: 1.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-06-01

1. RETENTION
Adult health records are retained for 8 years after the last episode of care; audit logs for 6 years. Analytical extracts are deleted when the analysis ends.

2. DE-IDENTIFICATION
Data used for analytics or AI models is pseudonymised: direct identifiers (names, social security numbers, contact details) are replaced with tokens, dates of birth reduced to year, and precise locations generalised.

3. INCIDENT RESPONSE
Any suspected breach (lost device, misdirected record, exposed credential) is reported to the information security team within 1 hour. The team contains the incident, assesses the risk to patients, and notifies the regulator within 72 hours when required.
