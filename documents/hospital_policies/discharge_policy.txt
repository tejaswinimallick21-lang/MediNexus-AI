DOCUMENT TITLE: Inpatient Discharge Planning and Transitional Care Policy
DOCUMENT ID: POL-HOSP-001
REVISION: 3.2
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-01-01

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy outlines standardized procedures for safe, effective, and coordinated discharge of adult inpatients across all MediNexus healthcare facilities to mitigate preventable 30-day readmissions.

2. MULTIDISCIPLINARY DISCHARGE ROUNDS
Every inpatient care team must initiate discharge planning within 24 hours of admission.
Daily interdisciplinary rounds at 09:00 AM must identify patients anticipated for discharge within 48 hours. Target time for physical discharge departure is 11:00 AM to facilitate incoming bed availability.

3. HIGH-RISK READMISSION PATHWAY
Patients identified as 'High Risk' by the clinical predictive intelligence system (probability >= 70%) must be placed on the Enhanced Transitional Protocol:
- Clinical Nurse Specialist phone follow-up within 48 hours of discharge.
- In-person outpatient specialist or primary care follow-up appointment secured within 7 days.
- Discharge summary transmitted electronically to primary provider within 12 hours.
- Direct-to-bedside delivery of 30-day essential maintenance medications.

4. MEDICATION RECONCILIATION
The attending physician and clinical pharmacist must conduct independent medication reconciliations:
- Review pre-admission drug regimen against inpatient treatment modifications.
- Explicitly annotate discontinued medications with clinical rationale.
- Educate the patient or caregiver regarding new dosages, timing, and adverse reaction indicators.
