DOCUMENT TITLE: Medical Equipment Maintenance Policy
DOCUMENT ID: POL-OPS-004
REVISION: 1.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-06-01

1. PLANNED PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE
Every device has a service interval from the manufacturer. Imaging (CT, MRI) is serviced every 90 days, lab analysers every 60 days, infusion pumps annually.

2. OVERDUE EQUIPMENT
Equipment more than 30 days past its service date is labelled "Do Not Use" and removed from clinical areas until serviced.

3. FAILURE REPORTING
Staff report any failure through the biomedical engineering helpdesk. Critical devices (ventilators, defibrillators) have a 4-hour response target.
