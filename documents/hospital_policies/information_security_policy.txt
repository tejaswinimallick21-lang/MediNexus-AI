DOCUMENT TITLE: Information Security and Access Control Policy
DOCUMENT ID: POL-IT-001
REVISION: 1.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-06-01

1. ACCESS CONTROL
Access to systems is role-based and limited to what the job requires (least privilege). Access is reviewed every 90 days and removed on the day a staff member leaves.

2. PASSWORDS AND ACCOUNTS
Passwords are at least 12 characters. Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts. Shared accounts are prohibited; demonstration credentials must be changed before any system is shared outside the development team.

3. AUDIT
Access to patient records, data exports and administrative actions are logged and the logs kept for at least 6 years. Unusual access patterns are reviewed weekly.

4. SECRETS
API keys and passwords must never be stored in source code or committed to version control. They are kept in a secrets store or local environment file excluded from version control, and rotated at least annually or immediately if exposed.
