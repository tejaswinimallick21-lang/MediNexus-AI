DOCUMENT TITLE: Hand Hygiene and Standard Precautions Policy
DOCUMENT ID: POL-IPC-001
REVISION: 1.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-06-01

1. WHEN TO CLEAN HANDS (FIVE MOMENTS)
Staff must clean hands before touching a patient, before a clean or aseptic procedure, after body-fluid exposure risk, after touching a patient, and after touching patient surroundings.

2. METHOD
- Alcohol-based hand rub is the default for visibly clean hands; rub all surfaces for 20-30 seconds until dry.
- Soap and water (40-60 seconds) is required when hands are visibly soiled and after caring for patients with suspected Clostridioides difficile or norovirus, because alcohol does not reliably inactivate spores.

3. GLOVES
Gloves do not replace hand hygiene. Remove gloves and clean hands between patients and between dirty and clean tasks on the same patient.

4. COMPLIANCE MONITORING
Each ward is audited monthly by direct observation. The compliance target is 90% or higher; wards below 80% for two consecutive months receive a focused improvement plan.
