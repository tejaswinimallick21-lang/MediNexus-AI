DOCUMENT TITLE: Transmission-Based Isolation Precautions
DOCUMENT ID: POL-IPC-002
REVISION: 1.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-06-01

1. CONTACT PRECAUTIONS
Used for MRSA, VRE, CRE and C. difficile. Single room where possible, gown and gloves on entry, dedicated equipment such as stethoscopes and blood pressure cuffs.

2. DROPLET PRECAUTIONS
Used for influenza and pertussis. Surgical mask within close range of the patient; patient wears a mask during transport.

3. AIRBORNE PRECAUTIONS
Used for tuberculosis, measles and varicella. Negative-pressure airborne infection isolation room, fit-tested N95 respirator for staff, door kept closed.

4. DISCONTINUATION
Precautions are stopped only on the instruction of the infection prevention team, according to organism-specific clearance criteria.
