DOCUMENT TITLE: Adult Sepsis Screening and Escalation Protocol
DOCUMENT ID: CPG-IPC-004
REVISION: 1.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-06-01

1. WHO IS SCREENED
All adult emergency admissions and any inpatient with a new suspected infection are screened at triage and whenever vital signs deteriorate.

2. SCREENING TRIGGERS
Escalate when infection is suspected AND two or more of: respiratory rate 22 per minute or higher, altered mental status, systolic blood pressure 100 mmHg or lower, or a lactate of 2 mmol/L or higher.

3. FIRST-HOUR BUNDLE
Once sepsis is suspected the team should, within one hour: measure lactate, take blood cultures before antibiotics, start broad-spectrum antibiotics per the stewardship guide, begin fluid resuscitation for hypotension or lactate of 4 mmol/L or more, and start vasopressors if hypotension persists.

4. ESCALATION
The nurse in charge notifies the responsible physician immediately and the critical care outreach team if there is no response within 30 minutes or the patient deteriorates.
