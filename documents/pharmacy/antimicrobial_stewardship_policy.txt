DOCUMENT TITLE: Antimicrobial Stewardship Policy
DOCUMENT ID: POL-PHARM-003
REVISION: 1.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-06-01

1. PRINCIPLES
Start smart, then focus: prescribe promptly when infection is likely, document indication and planned duration, and review at 48-72 hours.

2. 48-72 HOUR REVIEW
At review the prescriber chooses one of: stop, switch from intravenous to oral, change to a narrower agent based on cultures, continue with a new review date, or move to outpatient therapy.

3. RESTRICTED ANTIMICROBIALS
Carbapenems, linezolid and IV colistin require approval from the infectious diseases or microbiology team within 24 hours of the first dose.

4. AUDIT
The stewardship pharmacist audits antimicrobial use monthly and reports days of therapy per 1,000 patient-days by ward.
