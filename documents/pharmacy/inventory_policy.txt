DOCUMENT TITLE: Pharmacy Inventory Replenishment and Critical Shortage Protocol
DOCUMENT ID: POL-PHARM-002
REVISION: 2.2
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-01-01

1. REORDER THRESHOLD DETERMINATION
Reorder thresholds are computed dynamically based on the formula:
Reorder Point = (Lead Time in Days * Average Daily Consumption) + Safety Buffer Stock (14 days minimum).

2. CRITICAL SHORTAGE AND STOCKOUT MANAGEMENT
When days of supply fall below 7 days or stock drops below 50% of the reorder point (critical stockout):
- The Pharmacy Information System automatically flags the item as 'High Risk'.
- The Clinical Pharmacy Director convenes therapeutic alternative review within 4 hours.
- Wholesaler secondary distributor expedited orders must be authorized immediately.
- Ward stock levels are reduced to 24-hour rations to prevent hoarding.

3. PHYSICAL AUDIT CYCLES
A comprehensive ABC cycle count is conducted weekly for top 20% high-volume and high-cost formulary items. Discrepancies exceeding 2% must be investigated for diversion or unrecorded wastage.
