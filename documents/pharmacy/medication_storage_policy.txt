DOCUMENT TITLE: Inpatient Medication Storage and Cold Chain Integrity Policy
DOCUMENT ID: POL-PHARM-001
REVISION: 3.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-01-01

1. TEMPERATURE CONTROL STANDARDS
- Refrigerated Medications (e.g., Insulin Glargine, Ceftriaxone reconstituted vials): Maintain strictly between 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
- Room Temperature Formulations (e.g., Metformin, Lisinopril, Atorvastatin): Store between 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F) with excursion tolerance up to 30°C.
- Continuous temperature monitoring devices log ambient readings every 15 minutes. Automatic audible and SMS alerts trigger upon continuous 30-minute deviation.

2. HIGH-ALERT MEDICATIONS
High-alert medications including concentrated electrolytes, IV anticoagulants, and neuromuscular blockers must be stored in segregated, brightly labeled bins in automated dispensing cabinets with secondary witness verification required for removal.

3. EXPIRED DRUG PROTOCOL
Any medication within 30 days of manufacturer expiration must be physically quarantined in the red disposal bin and logged in the inventory management system.
